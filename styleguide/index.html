Nothing in this palette fails 4.5:1 at body size — including accent, which measures 5.70:1 on --page-bg and 5.09:1 on --page-surface, both above the AA threshold. The restriction to large-scale use (pull lines, numerals, nav mark, live-phone outline; never under 18px) is a hierarchy decision, not a contrast failure — flagging that the math and the stated assumption diverge, in case that changes how tightly you want it enforced.
